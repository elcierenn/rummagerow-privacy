Privacy Policy

Rummage Row · Cozmoss Games · Effective date: 5 October 2026

Rummage Row ("the game") is made by Cozmoss Games ("we", "us"). The game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-game purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your save slots, game settings (sound, display, language) and progress (for example, whether you finished the practice day and which achievements you unlocked) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Stores and platforms

When you buy or download the game, the store you use handles your account and payment under its own privacy policy: Google Play, Apple App Store or Steam (Valve). On Steam, your achievements and save files may be handled by Steam's own features. We only see the information these stores make available to developers, such as aggregated sales numbers and, if you choose to share them with developers, crash reports.

3. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

4. Children

Rummage Row is a cozy game about running a flea market stall. Its casino mini-games (blackjack and coin toss) are played only with in-game money; no real money is ever involved and in-game money cannot be bought. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about privacy (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), email us and we will reply within 30 days.

6. Contact

Cozmoss Games, Türkiye · hello@cozmossgames.com

7. Changes

If the game ever starts handling personal data (for example, an online feature), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
